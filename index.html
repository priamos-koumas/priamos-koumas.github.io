Selected research & analysis

Nonprofit intake system

Process improvement · 2024

The problem

A nonprofit was processing client intake by hand. Records were duplicated across staff, information was slow to reach the people who needed it, and no one trusted the numbers they were reporting.

What I did

Interviewed staff to map how intake actually ran, made the case for replacing it, and delivered a new system on Salesforce — then documented the workflow so the team could run it without me.

Manual processing time down roughly 60%. Duplicate records eliminated.

Healthcare analytics dashboard

Business intelligence · 2025–26

The problem

A health dataset covering patient demographics and risk factors that nobody could act on — inconsistent, unvalidated, and impenetrable to a non-technical audience as it stood.

What I did

Cleaned and audited the data before drawing a single conclusion, then built reporting that surfaces the trends plainly and is explicit about where the analysis has limits.

Diagnostic classification study

Data analysis · 2025

The problem

What I did
